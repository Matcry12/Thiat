## Boss list and structure (attacks per boss, phases, enrage, arena, fight length)

### Takeaway
Boss attack sets are roughly 4-9 distinct moves per boss (from the few bosses documented), mostly bullet-pattern based. No HP thresholds, enrage rules, arena sizes or fight lengths were found.

### Cited Findings
- Hidden boss Blobulord (水滴大王) has about 6 attacks: slow jumping that leaves slime trails; random bullet spray; sweeping bullet stream; jumping slime bullets ("extremely hard to predict"); ground slam releasing ring bullets; teleport to 5 sewer grates and fire bullets that ricochet randomly off walls — [Xiaomi post](https://game.xiaomi.com/viewpoint/1391913026_1636934270643_100)
- Old King (hidden level, Abbey of the True Gun) has about 9 listed moves: curse fire plus fast random bullets; oil bottle drops with random cross-fire; elliptical 4-direction volleys with small gaps; 12-direction spreads that expand then reconverge; a giant projectile splitting into 64 bullets; full-coverage rotating patterns; tracking curse-fire rings; spiral flames — [Xiaomi post](https://game.xiaomi.com/viewpoint/1391913026_1636934270643_100)
- Black Stache (Criminal's Past) is described by the same author as "watery" design: fixed attack ranges and telegraphs, easy to avoid — [Xiaomi post](https://game.xiaomi.com/viewpoint/1391913026_1636934270643_100)
- The Dead Cells crossover "Giant" has four attacks: roar, hand impact waves, hand bullet barrages, eye lasers; reviewer found it underwhelming — [Xiaomi post](https://game.xiaomi.com/viewpoint/1410444434_1640271935056_16)
- Goblin Priest: low health, meteorite (with some targeting) plus barrage combo — search summary of [Fandom Tactics](https://soul-knight.fandom.com/wiki/Goblin_Priest_(Haunted)/Tactics)
- Devil's Snare stays in the arena center and constantly fires in all directions; pillars give cover — search summary of Fandom boss guides ([thread](https://soul-knight.fandom.com/f/p/4400000000000038937))
- Giant Crystal Crab: has a defensive form when it stops moving (it regenerates); surprise spike attacks; arena has covers/corners to play around — search summary of [Fandom Tactics](https://soul-knight.fandom.com/wiki/Giant_Crystal_Crab/Tactics)
- Varkolyn Leader is poison-based (poison ground patches; rogue dash hops them); melee bullet-bounce and high shield recommended — search summary of [itemlevel.net](https://itemlevel.net/soul-knight-prequel-all-weapon-boss-void-varkolyn-guide/) (may be about the Prequel game; unverified)

### Inferences
- Bosses are built as "bullet pattern menus": each move is a distinct geometry (ring, spread, stream, ricochet), chosen semi-randomly. Not confirmed as random vs scripted order.
- Bosses appear to sit in the centre or teleport (Devil's Snare, Blobulord), so arena is a single room with pillars/cover.

## Telegraphs

### Takeaway
Little confirmed. Chinese analysis says weak bosses (Black Stache) have fixed, readable telegraphs; the design emphasis is on slow bullets and spacing rather than long wind-ups. Some attacks are deliberately un-telegraphed/random.

### Cited Findings
- Colossus Zulan uses a delayed area laser (delay = the warning) — [Xiaomi post](https://game.xiaomi.com/viewpoint/1359077112_1638763783095_9)
- Author says do not try to predict Blobulord's ricochet bullets; rely on distance and raw skill — i.e. randomness, not a telegraph, is the challenge — [Xiaomi post](https://game.xiaomi.com/viewpoint/1391913026_1636934270643_100)
- Bosses react to player habits, e.g. attack when the player backs off to drink a potion — [Xiaomi post](https://game.xiaomi.com/viewpoint/1359077112_1638763783095_9)
- Design guide suggests picking attack by distance: far = pounce, mid/close = bite or combo, very close = sweep — [Xiaomi post](https://game.xiaomi.com/viewpoint/1359077112_1638763783095_9) (generic advice, not verified as ChillyRoom's method)

### Inferences
- The known "red ground marker / aim line before a laser" style is common in the game but I could NOT source telegraph durations or visuals. Treat as unverified.

### Gaps
- Wind-up durations, exclamation marks, flash colours, laser line timing: none found.

## Bullet patterns and speeds

### Takeaway
Patterns confirmed: rings, spreads (12-direction), streams, ricochet, splitting bullet (64 shards), rotating/spiral, tracking rings, ground puddles. Entry-level bosses have slow, low-damage bullets.

### Cited Findings
- "Ballistic" (弹道类) bosses such as Mandragora and Goblin Priest are the entry tier: slow bullets, low damage — [Xiaomi post](https://game.xiaomi.com/viewpoint/1359077112_1638763783095_9)
- Laser-type bosses (Grand Wizard, Colossus Zulan): very high damage, lasers cannot be blocked by melee weapons — [Xiaomi post](https://game.xiaomi.com/viewpoint/1359077112_1638763783095_9)
- Melee weapons can bounce/deflect normal bullets (implied by tips: "bounce bullets with melee") — search summary of [itemlevel.net](https://itemlevel.net/soul-knight-prequel-all-weapon-boss-void-varkolyn-guide/) (unverified for original game)
- Old King patterns listed above (12-way, 64-split, elliptical 4-way) — [Xiaomi post](https://game.xiaomi.com/viewpoint/1391913026_1636934270643_100)

## Melee and charge enemies

### Takeaway
Skeleton King is the cited example: has a dash/charge to chase the player; advice is keep distance, or circle around walls if it closes in.

### Cited Findings
- Mobility-type bosses (e.g. Skeleton King) use gap-closing dash skills; evade by spacing, or circle around wall blocks — [Xiaomi post](https://game.xiaomi.com/viewpoint/1359077112_1638763783095_9); search result confirming same at [Xiaomi](https://game.xiaomi.com/viewpoint/1359077112_1638763783095_9)

## Fairness and difficulty

### Takeaway
Survival is movement plus armor/shield buffer, per community. One credible critique: some hidden-boss moves rely on randomness and are near-unavoidable in certain positions.

### Cited Findings
- Author criticises Old King's Technical-5 pattern as nearly unavoidable when the player is above the boss, needing a block skill — [Xiaomi post](https://game.xiaomi.com/viewpoint/1391913026_1636934270643_100)
- "Badass" mode boss rush exists; guide blog on Fandom (not read) — [GrandWiz05 blog](https://soul-knight.fandom.com/wiki/User_blog:GrandWiz05/A_Guide_to_Badass_Boss_Rush)
- Shield of 6+ recommended vs Varkolyn Leader — [itemlevel.net](https://itemlevel.net/soul-knight-prequel-all-weapon-boss-void-varkolyn-guide/) (Prequel? unverified)

## Suggested lessons for BossFight (inferred, not Soul Knight facts)
- Menu of distinct geometric bullet patterns per boss (ring, spread, stream, ricochet) is the proven cheap recipe.
- Keep a mix of fully telegraphed moves and a few high-randomness moves; critics only complain when randomness leaves no safe spot.
- Slow bullets for early bosses; delayed area lasers as the high-damage tier.
