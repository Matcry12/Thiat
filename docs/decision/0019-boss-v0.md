# 0019 — The v0 boss

## Context
Slice 4 asks: *is losing to the boss fair?* Every death must be explainable in one
sentence ("I dodged too early"). The boss needs 3 attacks, a phase 2, an enrage
rule, and a brain that can be swapped for an RL policy later ([0005](0005-architecture.md),
[0008](0008-rl-inference.md)).

## Decision
- **Attack = one small node.** `scripts/boss/boss_attack.gd` (`BossAttack`) is the base:
  TELEGRAPH -> ACTIVE -> RECOVERY, a cooldown, `damage` / `parryable` / times as `@export`.
  `slam_attack.gd`, `spread_attack.gd`, `charge_attack.gd`, `ring_attack.gd` extend it and sit
  under `Boss/Attacks` in `boss.tscn`, each with its own `Hitbox` + `Telegraph` children.
  `boss.gd` finds every `BossAttack` child by itself. A new attack = one script + one node;
  nothing else is edited (the F3 panel and F1 overlay find it too).
- **Reuse, no forks.** Hit areas are the `Hitbox` shapes authored in `boss.tscn` (0011); each
  `Telegraph` takes its size from that same shape. `Telegraph` got one new branch (convex polygon)
  for the spread fan. `Projectile.setup()` got an optional `hit_mask` (boss bolts hit layer 2, the
  player). Purple = `parryable=false` (slam, ring), orange = parryable (spread, charge).
- **Brain boundary.** `boss.gd` builds a plain `state` Dictionary (`hp_fraction, phase,
  player_distance, player_angle, player_weapon, cooldowns, enraged`) and asks
  `Brain.choose_action(state) -> String`. Answers: an attack name, `"chase"`, `"idle"`. An answer
  that is unknown, on cooldown or locked by phase is a no-op. `boss.gd` never names
  `ScriptedBrain` (a test reads the source to keep it so). `set_brain()` swaps it at runtime.
- **Stagger.** `Boss.stagger(duration)` (called duck-typed by `Guard` on a parry) cancels the
  running attack (its cooldown still starts), freezes movement and decisions for `duration`.
- **Phase 2** at 60% hp (sticky). One new move, `ring`: a purple blast on the player's spot, then
  a bigger purple blast on the same spot after a second warning. Dodging the first is not enough;
  you must also leave the centre.
- **Enrage.** The arena has a `FightZone` (an `Area2D`, rectangle drawn in the scene; resize it
  in the editor). Player outside it for more than `enrage_delay` (3 s) -> `enraged`: the brain
  answers `charge` every time, and the charge is 2x speed with a 0.3 s cooldown. Back inside ->
  calm. The visual is told by the `enraged_changed` signal; combat never touches it.
- **Charge is stopped by the player's body.** No collision exception: the dash ends at the first
  wall or at the player's body, so the two bodies never end up overlapped and the player cannot
  walk through the boss. The charge Hitbox (r 36) reaches the player's hurtbox before the bodies
  touch (39 px) and stays on 3 frames after the dash, so the hit is never missed.
- **Bolts.** Each bolt is its own damage source (`setup(..., damage_source)`): a parry destroys
  that bolt and the arc check uses the bolt's real position. It does **not** stagger the boss.
  Only a parried charge does. A bolt ignores bodies whose Hurtbox is on a layer it does not
  target (the training Dummy is not a shield), and live bolts are freed when the boss dies.
  Bolts are orange (parryable colour family).
- **Ring** shows its bigger second circle, faint, from the start of the first warning.
- **Enraged brain** answers `charge` when it is ready and `chase` otherwise (never stands still).
  How close the boss walks is the brain's call (`chase_stop_range`); the body has no stop distance.
- **Death and restart.** `player.dead` is set from `Health.died` and ends all input.
  `EndScreen` shows "You died - R to retry" / "Boss defeated - R to retry"; `R` (`restart`
  action) reloads the arena. The first ending wins.
- **Test arena.** The boss is in `arena.tscn`; the Dummy stays (moved to (700,130), off the
  boss-to-player line, still to the right of the player because parry-arc tests rely on that).
  The boss has a plain `passive` var (not an `@export`, code owns it): true = stands still, no
  attacks. The 22 older tests that load the arena set `passive = true` on their first line; the
  boss tests use a stub Brain and `force_next()`.

## Why
The player-facing question is fairness, so every attack tells you what it will do (shape,
colour, time) and how to answer it (orange = parry, purple = dodge). Small attack nodes keep
"add a fifth attack" cheap. A dumb, well-defined brain boundary is what makes the RL boss a
drop-in later.

## Consequences
- All numbers are starting values. Most need a playtest: slam/ring telegraph times (fairness),
  damage (35/25/30/15), enrage delay and zone size, stagger length (0.8 s from Guard), brain ranges.
- The scripted boss never retreats: it walks to `chase_stop_range` and stays. A "retreat" answer
  can be added to the brain later without touching `boss.gd` (unknown answers are already no-ops
  and `chase`/`idle` are the only movement).
- The spread telegraph is the whole fan wedge; the gaps between the 5 bolts are safe spots.
- The charge lane is drawn to full `distance`; it does not shorten for a wall.
